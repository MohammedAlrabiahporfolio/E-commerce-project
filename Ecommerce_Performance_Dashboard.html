Insights & recommendations

Findings from the full 2021–2023 sample

  1. 01

    Electronics led in completed sales

    Electronics generated 622,636 in completed sales, 22.5% of the total.

    I’d check gross profit as well as sales before expanding Electronics promotions.

  2. 02

    Home had the highest gross margin

    Home reached 24.72%, compared with 22.12% for Clothing.

    I’d compare pricing, costs, and discounts before testing promotions on higher-margin products.

  3. 03

    Returns and cancellations

    Of 2,585 orders, 247 were returned (9.6%) and 122 were cancelled (4.7%).

    I’d look for patterns by city, category, and payment method before deciding what to change.

  4. 04

    Discounts and loss-making lines

    277 completed lines generated a combined gross loss of 5,798.

    I’d review those sales and set discount limits based on each product’s cost and target margin.

Source: E-commerce workbook · 6,000 order lines · 1 Jan 2021–19 Jun 2023

Metric definitions

Financial metrics cover completed orders. Gross profit is sales after discounts less product cost. Orders and customers are counted once per selected view. Return and cancellation rates use all orders. Currency was not specified in the source.
